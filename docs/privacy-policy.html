Privacy Policy

Whiskerwood Library · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Whiskerwood Library (“the game”, “we”) is a casual idle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it short and honest.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, floors, books, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts, we do not upload your save, and we have no servers that receive it. Uninstalling the game or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional; you choose to watch them for in-game bonuses. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about ad interactions, app usage and diagnostics (e.g. crashes).

This data is collected by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example “your coin jar is full”). They are created on the device and no information is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game’s settings or your phone’s settings.

4. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

5. Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising services described above, we do not share information with third parties.

7. Security

Data sent by the advertising SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

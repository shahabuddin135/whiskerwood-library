A cozy idle game about mice, books and a very sleepy dragon

Whiskerwood Library

Grandma Mimsy left you a tiny library in the meadow. Grow it floor by floor, season by season, into a whole mouse city, all the way down to a dragon who's been waiting four hundred years for a bedtime story.

What you'll do

From one little room to a whole city

Every reader drops a coin in the jar. Spend them, grow, and discover what's hidden under the floorboards.

Build it up, floor by floor

Stack ten magical floors on your library, each with its own look, furniture and books to collect: the Storybook Corner, a café, the Adventure Map Room, a spooky Mystery Attic, a Stargazer Observatory, the Moonlit Music Hall and the Grand Atrium.

  • Armchairs, bookshelves, lamps and rugs to upgrade
  • A 50-book album to fill
  • Move to Windmill Hill, Lighthouse Cove, Willow Pond and Moonlit Castle for bigger libraries

A little world full of life

Dozens of tiny readers come and go, chat, gossip about books and ask for help finding them. Tap glowing guests before they leave: celebrities, authors signing books, even Queen Mozzarella.

  • Shoo Baroness Clawdia, the biggest cat in the meadow, away from the windows
  • Catch Sneaky Rattigan the coin thief, squish bookworms, impress a fussy critic
  • Earn while you're away, then come back to a full coin jar

Cafés, club nights and a dinosaur museum

Open wings beside the tower, each one bustling with its own visitors and staff, and grow each one up to three storeys.

  • The Crumb Café: trays of warm scones
  • The Community Hall: book club, choir, chess and dance nights
  • The Inky Paw Printing Press: print books and read the daily Gazette
  • The Bone & Bauble Museum: show off the fossils you dig up

Dig beneath the roots

A brass trapdoor under the vault leads to the Deep Stacks, a forgotten library buried in the soil. Send the mole crew digging through the maze to find fossils and treasure, and restore eight lost halls.

  • A Triceratops skull, a Pterodactyl, a Sabre-toothed cat…
  • Each restored hall reveals a page of the Chronicle, a real mystery to read

Ride the lift to Mole City

Up top, a city of mice. Down below, a city of moles. The Grand Lift carries mice, hamsters and moles from a little lift house on the lawn all the way down.

  • Eight mole businesses to open and level up: a pie shop, a glow-worm cinema, a mud spa, a cheese cave, a jeweller and more
  • Every shop fills with customers as it grows

Wake the dragon

At the very bottom, behind a door with eight locks, sleeps Umber, keeper of the Mighty Tales. Open the door, answer his question, and bring the book that remembers every story home.

  • Doubles your library's income, forever
  • Then read him a bedtime story every night. He always asks for the one about the King's socks.
All year round

Seasons, weather and festivals

Spring blossoms, summer sunflowers, autumn leaves and winter snow, with rainy days, rainbows and fireflies at night. And there's always something to celebrate.

14 festivals through the year, including Ramadan and Eid, Hanukkah, New Year, Book Week and your library's own birthday.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. {{NAME}} is free to play. The Android app shows ads, and rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one for a bonus. The web version has no ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, you can play without a connection, and your library keeps earning while you're away.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your own device. There's no sign-up and nothing to log in to.

What data does the game collect?

We don't collect your personal data ourselves. In the Android app, Google AdMob serves the ads and processes some device data to do so. The privacy policy explains exactly what, and how to control it.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer, and any modern web browser at /play.

Is it suitable for children?

It's a gentle game for everyone, but it isn't specifically designed for children under 13, because the Android app shows ads.

How do I get help or delete my data?

Email {{EMAIL}}. Your progress lives only on your device, so you can delete it with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by uninstalling. See deleting your data.

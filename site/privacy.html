1. Your game data stays on your device

Your progress (coins, floors, books, settings and so on) is saved only in the game's storage on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We don't upload your save, and we have no servers that receive it. Because we never receive it, we can't see it, recover it, or move it to another device.

2. Advertising in the Android app (Google AdMob)

The Android app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. The app may also show an occasional full-screen ad between play sessions. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location that can be derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as crash data.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We don't receive this data in a form that identifies you. We only see overall reports, such as how many ads were shown.

3. Your ad choices

  • Consent: if you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (and anywhere else the law requires it), the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads, and you choose. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone). After that, apps get a string of zeros instead.
  • Non-personalised ads: if you don't consent, or you delete your ID, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device, for example when your coin jar is full. They're created on the device itself, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

6. The web version and this website

The web version at /play has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Its progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your device, and you can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

This website and the web version are hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. It keeps this in short-lived server logs. See Vercel's Privacy Policy. We don't use this information to identify you.

7. Other Google Play features

The Android app may ask whether you'd like to rate it, using Google Play's in-app review feature. Google Play handles that entirely. We don't see who rated or what they wrote in the app, only the public reviews on Google Play. The game doesn't currently sell anything. If in-app purchases are ever added, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we'll never see your payment details.

8. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for any purpose other than showing ads through Google AdMob in the Android app, as described above.

9. Children

The game is designed for a general audience. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

10. Deleting your data

  • Your progress: use Settings → Reset progress in the game, uninstall the Android app, or (for the web version) clear this site's data in your browser. That deletes your data completely, because we hold no copy.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your Advertising ID (see section 3). Google's data controls are at myaccount.google.com.
  • Questions or requests: email {{EMAIL}}.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or limit how it's used. We don't hold personal information about you on our own systems. For advertising data, use the controls above or contact Google. If you have any question or request, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

12. Security and international transfers

Data sent by the advertising service and by the website is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google and Vercel may process data in countries other than your own, under their own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
